Form activation checklist

1. Revoke the formerly public Brevo key in Brevo. Removing it from source does
   not revoke it or remove copies from earlier deployments/history.
2. Set new BREVO_API_KEY and TURNSTILE_SECRET_KEY with wrangler secret put.
3. Set TURNSTILE_SITE_KEY (public), BREVO_SENDER_EMAIL (verified sender),
   CONTACT_TO_EMAIL (recipient), BREVO_LIST_ID (newsletter list).
4. Replace the pending privacy pages with owner-approved notices, including
   controller identity/contact, purposes, legal bases, retention, processors,
   rights and withdrawal/unsubscribe information. Only then set PRIVACY_APPROVED=true.
5. Serve /api/* from functions/api/_worker.js on the same origin as public/.
   A static-only preview cannot process forms and will display unavailable status.
6. Test configured forms in staging before deployment, including real delivery,
   captcha failures and newsletter unsubscribe behavior. No real email was sent
   during implementation tests.
7. Supply the official HelloAsso campaign URL to replace the contact fallback.
8. Confirm the displayed 150+ member figure and official DevFest name.

API documentation:
https://developers.brevo.com/reference/send-transac-email
